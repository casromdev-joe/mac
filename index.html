What We Do

Our Services

From ground prep to final seal, MAC Contracting handles every phase of concrete and masonry work with the skill and equipment your project demands.

🏗️

Commercial Concrete

Full-scale commercial concrete services for retail, industrial, and mixed-use projects. Foundations, slabs, curb & gutter, site flatwork, and more.

  • Foundations and footings
  • Slab-on-grade construction
  • Curb and gutter installation
  • Site preparation and grading coordination
  • Tenant improvement concrete
  • Drive-through and parking structures
🏠

Residential Concrete

Quality concrete work for homeowners and property managers. Driveways, patios, walkways, and pool decks—built to last and look great.

  • Driveway installation and replacement
  • Patio slabs and pool decks
  • Walkways and sidewalks
  • Steps and retaining walls
  • Garage floors and aprons
  • Decorative concrete finishes
♿

ADA Improvements

Keep your property accessible and code-compliant. We install detectable warning surfaces, ADA curb ramps, accessible pathways, and signage foundations.

  • ADA-compliant curb ramp installation
  • Detectable warning surface (DWS) panels
  • Accessible pedestrian route construction
  • Cross-slope grading to 2% max
  • Bollard and signage foundations
  • ADA parking space and striping prep
🔧

Concrete Repair & Resurfacing

Cracked, sunken, or deteriorated concrete? We assess, repair, and resurface existing flatwork to extend life and restore appearance.

  • Crack and joint repair
  • Slab leveling and mudjacking
  • Surface resurfacing and overlays
  • Concrete sealing and resealing
  • Sidewalk trip hazard removal
  • Spall and scaling repair
🧱

Masonry & Block Work

Structural and decorative masonry including CMU block walls, retaining structures, planters, and block foundations for residential and commercial properties.

  • CMU block wall construction
  • Retaining walls
  • Planters and landscape edging
  • Pilasters and entry columns
  • Masonry repair and repointing
  • Stone and brick veneer
📐

Site Prep & New Construction

We partner with developers and general contractors on new construction projects—from rough grading and underground prep to flatwork and final concrete scope.

  • Site preparation and excavation coordination
  • Underground utility trench backfill
  • Form and pour for new structures
  • GC preferred subcontractor
  • Large-scale commercial projects

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Answers to what most customers ask before getting started with us.

Do you offer free estimates?
+
Yes. We provide free, no-obligation estimates for all projects. Fill out our quote request form or give us a call and we'll schedule a site visit to assess your project and provide a detailed written estimate.
Do you handle both commercial and residential projects?
+
Absolutely. We work on everything from residential driveways and patio slabs to large-scale commercial foundations, tenant improvements, and public works projects. No project is too small or too large.
Are you licensed and insured?
+
Yes. MAC Contracting is a licensed California General Engineering Contractor with a valid CSLB license. We carry full general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage. Certificates of Insurance are available upon request.
What areas do you serve?
+
We serve all of Los Angeles County, Riverside County, San Bernardino County, and Orange County. Our crew is based in Azusa, CA, putting us in an ideal location to reach projects across the Inland Empire, San Gabriel Valley, and surrounding areas.
How soon can you start a project?
+
Start dates depend on project scope and our current schedule. For small to mid-size jobs, we can often begin within 1–3 weeks of contract execution. Contact us to discuss your specific timeline.
Can I upload photos when requesting a quote?
+
Yes. You can send photos to our email at info@maccontracting.com along with your project details. Photos of existing conditions help us provide a more accurate estimate.
Do you work with general contractors?
+
Yes, and it's a large part of our business. MAC Contracting is a preferred concrete subcontractor for general contractors and developers across Southern California. We understand GC workflows, scheduling demands, and documentation requirements.
What types of concrete work do you specialize in?
+
Our specialties include commercial concrete, residential flatwork, foundations and footings, ADA-compliant site improvements, curb and gutter, driveways and parking areas, masonry and CMU block work, concrete repair and resurfacing, and sealing/resealing. If it involves concrete or masonry, we do it.
